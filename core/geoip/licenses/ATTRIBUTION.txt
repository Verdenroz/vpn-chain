# Data sources

`geoip_ranges.bin` and `geoip_cities.tsv` — IPv4 range → country and city
table, compiled from the
[DB-IP Lite city database](https://db-ip.com/db/download/ip-to-city-lite)
(2026-10 edition), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution: IP Geolocation by [DB-IP](https://db-ip.com).
`tools/build_geoip.py` rebuilds both from the pinned, checksummed download.

`CountryGeo.kt` centroids — computed from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:110m Admin 0 Countries
(public domain, no attribution required), with a small manual fallback list
for territories the 110m layer omits.

The in-app map background (`world.svg`) is traced from Natural Earth 1:110m
Land polygons (public domain).
