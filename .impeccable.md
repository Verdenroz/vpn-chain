## Design Context

### Users
Solo, technically fluent user (the developer), on desktop Linux and Android. The
screen gets opened for one of two reasons: to turn the chain on or off, or to
answer "is my traffic actually going where I think it is, right now?" in a few
seconds. No onboarding, no explaining what an entry hop is.

### Brand Personality
Plain · clear · candid.

A well-made VPN app, not an object and not a toy. The state is readable from
across the room, the controls are where you expect them, and the words are short.
When protection is absent the screen says so.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Map and status bar, after the commercial VPN apps.**

- A full-width **status bar** carries the state: green "Connected", red "Not
  protected", neutral "Connecting". It reflects tunnel state only; the kill
  switch is a row in the card.
- A real, simplified **world map** is the centerpiece. The route is drawn on it:
  small light dots for origin and entry, a larger green dot with a soft halo for
  the exit, the connected leg solid green, a disconnected one dashed grey.
- A **bottom card** (side panel on desktop) holds the state line, exit country,
  hop rows (You, Entry, Exit, Rerouted), IP and kill switch, and two buttons:
  "Change" (outlined) and "Disconnect" / "Connect" (filled red / green).
- Phone: plain text tabs (Chain, Settings, Logs). Desktop: a left rail with the
  same destinations.
- **Follows the system theme**; light and dark are both designed, not one
  translated from the other. The user can still force either in Settings.
- **Hanken Grotesk** only, tabular digits for readouts. Radii: card 16, buttons
  10, sheet 20 (top).
- **Palette** (OKLCH, hue 250 neutrals, 150 green, 25 red, 65 amber):
  - Dark: background 0.25/0.04, card and rail 0.21/0.04, land 0.31/0.04, ink
    0.96, muted ink 0.77/0.02, connected 0.53/0.15, unprotected 0.57/0.17,
    disconnect 0.50/0.15, rerouted 0.80/0.13.
  - Light: background 0.94/0.012, card 0.995, land 0.985, ink 0.24/0.03, muted
    ink 0.46/0.02, connected 0.54/0.14, unprotected 0.52/0.16, rerouted
    0.66/0.13 (0.52/0.12 as text).
- White text on the green and red fills holds 4.5:1; check it after any change.
- Motion only reflects a state change. Nothing animates while idle.

Anti-references: neon "hacker" green on black, decorative glow, glassmorphism,
stock Material components with default purple.

### Copy
Short labels, no explanatory sentences, no wit. "Connected · 41 ms", "Kill
switch", "Change". Error and kill-switch reason text is shown as given and stays
selectable, since it can carry commands.

### Design Principles
1. **State first.** The bar and the button color say what is happening before
   anything is read.
2. **Readouts are measured, never assumed.** Configured values are shown as
   configured, in small muted text; unknown is shown as unknown.
3. **Never overstate protection.** "Not protected" is red and says so.
4. **Motion means a change.** Animate transitions between states, not idle time.
5. **Quiet chrome.** Color is reserved for state and the route; everything else
   is neutral.
